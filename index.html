About

I am a graduate of the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh (B.Sc. Engineering, 2019–2025). My research spans computational materials science, data-driven materials science, and experimental composite materials. My current computational research focuses on first-principles investigation of fluoroperovskites and perovskite hydrides, including their structural, electronic, mechanical, optical, thermal, and hydrogen-storage properties. I am also developing my research direction in materials informatics and machine learning for materials-property prediction. I am currently preparing for PhD opportunities for Spring/Fall 2027, particularly in computational and data-driven materials research.

Research Interests

My research interests center on computational and data driven materials science, with particular emphasis on understanding structure property relationships in energy, hydrogen-storage, optoelectronic, and composite materials through first-principles study, materials informatics, and experimental analysis.

Computational Materials Science

First-principles modeling of structural, electronic, mechanical, optical, and thermal properties to understand materials behavior at the atomic scale.

Data-Driven Materials Science

Machine learning approaches for materials-property prediction, generalization, and reliable modeling across inorganic materials systems.

Materials Informatics

Use of composition-based descriptors, materials datasets, and data analysis to uncover relationships between chemical composition and material properties.

Energy & Hydrogen-Storage Materials

Computational investigation of perovskite hydrides and related materials for hydrogen storage, with emphasis on composition-dependent physical properties.

Optoelectronic Materials

Investigation of electronic structure, band-gap tuning, and optical response in lead-free perovskite materials for potential optoelectronic applications.

Composite Materials

Experimental study of sustainable hybrid polymer composites, focusing on mechanical performance, microstructure, and the effects of natural and waste-derived constituents.
